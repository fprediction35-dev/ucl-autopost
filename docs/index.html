About the posting tool

"ucl-autopost" is a private tool used only by the owner of these accounts. It renders the simulation videos and uploads them to the owner's own accounts through the official APIs of each platform. It is not offered to other users and does not access anyone else's account or data.

Simulations are for entertainment. They are not real match results and are not betting advice. Not affiliated with UEFA or any club.
